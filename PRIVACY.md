# Glint Capture privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Glint Capture ("Glint") is a screen capture app published by dunit.io. It works entirely on your PC.

## What Glint accesses

To do its job, Glint accesses information on your PC that can include personal information:

- **Your screen.** When you take a capture, Glint saves an image of the area, window or screen you chose.
  That image can show anything that was on screen, such as names, email addresses or messages.
- **Window titles and app names.** Glint records which app and window a capture came from (for example
  "Chrome") so you can sort and filter your captures.
- **Text in your captures.** Glint reads the text inside your captures with the OCR built into Windows, so you
  can copy it and search your library.
- **Your settings**, such as shortcuts, folders, tags and theme.

## How it is used, stored and secured

- All of this is used only to provide Glint's features on your PC.
- It is stored only on your PC: captures in your save folder (by default `Pictures\Glint`), and settings,
  tags and the search index in Glint's own app-data folder. It is protected by your Windows account like
  your other files.
- Glint makes **no network connections**. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or cloud
  features, and it never uploads, sells or shares anything.

## Who it is shared with

Nobody. Glint does not disclose any information to dunit.io or to any other party. A capture only leaves
your PC if you copy, paste or send it somewhere yourself.

## Your controls

- You choose what to capture and where captures are saved (Settings > Save folder).
- You can delete any capture from Glint's library or from the save folder at any time.
- "Hide sensitive info" can blur emails, phone numbers, card numbers and keys in captures, and can be
  switched on to run automatically (Settings).
- Uninstalling Glint removes the app. Your captures stay in your save folder until you delete them.

## Microsoft Store

If you install Glint from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, updates and any ratings or
reviews under [its own privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if Glint's features change how information is handled. Questions:
