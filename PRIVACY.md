# Glint privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Glint is a screen capture app that works entirely on your PC.

- **No data leaves your PC.** Glint makes no network connections. It has no accounts, analytics,
  telemetry, advertising or cloud features.
- **Your captures stay where you save them.** Screenshots, edits, tags and the text Glint reads from your
  captures (for search) are stored only in your save folder and Glint's settings folder on your PC. You can
  delete them at any time.
- **Text recognition happens on your PC.** Glint uses the OCR built into Windows. Nothing is sent to an
  online service.
- **Glint does not collect personal information** and has nothing to share with or sell to anyone.

If you install Glint from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and updates under
[its own privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Jagjeet-Dhuna/Glint/issues.
